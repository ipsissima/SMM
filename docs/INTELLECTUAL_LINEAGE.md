# Intellectual lineage and public chronology

The introduction and bibliography of both manuscript source formats were updated on 26 November 2026. The date-specific original preprint links are important: arXiv's unversioned DOI resolves to the latest revision, and the arXiv abstract for v2 retains the v1 abstract despite the v2 full text documenting the astrocytic architecture. Consult [2024 v1 on bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.11.22.624908v1) and the [June 2025 v2 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12106v2), especially sections 1–3 and figure 1.

The chronology establishes public disclosure of the architecture, not priority over all preceding astrocyte network studies or evidence of influence. The Cooper July 2025 preprint and April 2026 Nature paper provide subsequent anatomical evidence for divergence from neuronal connectivity. The review by Oliveira et al. was received in October 2024, despite its May 2026 publication, so no priority claim against their conceptual development is warranted. The Cafiso preprint dates from June 2026 (before its November Brain Research publication), and the Nakatani–De Schutter bioRxiv DOI is 10.64898/2026.06.23.734112 (before their November eLife reviewed preprint). The latter's eLife history lists a later August preprint date; the earlier bioRxiv record is the appropriate reference for public chronology, not eLife publication alone.

The claims specific to the present paper are conditional operator recovery, topology discrimination against weighted shared-support alternatives, protocol-specific sensitivity and its electrodiffusive dependence. Earlier versions did not derive these particular mathematical results. The full-physics addenda must still be integrated before submission.
