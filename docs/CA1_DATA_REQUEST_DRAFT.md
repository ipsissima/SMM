# Draft request for the CA1 potassium dataset (not sent)

To: Christian Henneberger (christian.henneberger@uni-bonn.de; corresponding author listed in Glia article 10.1002/glia.23751)

Subject: Data request: CA1 extracellular potassium transients and gap-junction uncoupling

Dear Professor Henneberger,

I am developing a mathematical and computational framework for the relationship between astroglial transport geometry and spatial ionic responses, using the published electrodiffusive model of Sætra and Mori as the biophysical implementation. Your 2020 study with Breithausen and colleagues on potassium buffering in CA1 stratum radiatum is particularly relevant to our efforts to distinguish ionic transport mechanisms from their spatial organization.

Would it be possible to obtain the underlying extracellular potassium traces for the iontophoretic experiments in Figures 5 and 6, together with the corresponding stimulus currents and durations, source–recording distances, treatment and control labels, baseline concentrations, and any available electrode or slice geometry information? We would use these data to calibrate the coupled control conditions and evaluate the uncoupling predictions separately. Our present one-dimensional aggregate-data comparison underestimates the measured high-load uncoupling effect, so the individual traces and geometry would be extremely helpful for assessing the source of that discrepancy.

We would of course cite your study and respect the conditions you specify for access, reuse, and redistribution of the data.

Thank you very much for considering the request.

Best regards,
Andreu Ballús Santacana
Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona
