# SMM-II v0.18 — final paired-recording revision audit

## Scientific change
The earlier 20 pA assay used a provisional small-network shunt and conflated input resistance with relative geometry-associated transfer. The new calculation directly tests this criticism. It is an adverse focal-projection result and a limitation on the empirical calibration, not a failure of the exact two-sided reach theorem or native migration ablation. The paper no longer claims the paired-recording experiment independently detects the early full-operator window.

The primary design is 64 connected 31^3 periodic graphs, 29,791 sites each, seven ages P7,9,10,11,12,15,21, 32 whole graphs held out. Independent face increments preserve each weighted degree (=6), support, lengths and total budget; the increments have SD chosen by the synthetic design, not inferred from CA1. Other designs: 32 full 31^3 differential-return graphs at P11/P21; 32 half-amplitude 31^3 clamped graphs, 32 half-amplitude 15^3 clamped graphs, 32 full-amplitude 15^3 differential graphs (all seven ages for the latter three). There are 1,184 graph-age combinations; the same seed may pair corresponding graph designs and is not evidence of independent biological samples. Failed long execution attempts were terminated; no partial values are used. Primary and other completed outputs are the five `large_*` result JSON files (the earlier eight-graph exploratory run is not in the primary archive). `batch_direct.py` executes reproducible small batches; `collect_checkpoints.py` collects them. The 15^3 runs used the earlier equivalent Fourier-preconditioned clamped solver, verified against dense and tight-tolerance solutions; the final clamped solver can reproduce them with minor floating-point differences. 

## Resistance and relative response
Zhong et al., Glia 2023, DOI 10.1002/glia.24327, Fig. 5e2 and §3.5.2: passive astrocytes have a group Rin of 9.7±1.0 MOhm at P10–12, remaining low thereafter; P2–3 18.4±3.3 MOhm. P7/P9 interpolation is a design convention. The 10.4 MOhm VRA figure is for a separate population. A 6 MOhm P21 target is a sensitivity scenario, not an additional Zhong measurement. A fixed single P11 shunt fit (33.384 nS, clamped) gives mature 2.703 MOhm, incompatible with the reported PA plateau; differential return gives 2.903 MOhm. Age-specific output scaling requires 33.384 to 9.302 nS in the primary model (held-out 9.701/9.702 MOhm). This is a calibration of transfer units, not independent confirmation of the intrinsic membrane shunt or a new value of the native tissue gm. The shunt fit's age trajectory highlights a tension in the reference calibration and is not a claim of decreasing membrane conductance. 

Primary held-out relative RMS (%) at P11/P21, axial steps 1: 3.3518/4.4379; 2: .6706/1.3923; 3: .1142/.4075. All five ensembles retain P11<P21 across all three axial classes. At 200 pA and fixed P11 scale adjacent absolute RMS .06521/.02412 mV; with age-scaled resistance .06521/.08657; with 6 MOhm mature scenario .06521/.05355. Matching a 2 mV local deflection gives .06704/.08876 mV, .01341/.02785, .00228/.00815 for axial steps 1,2,3. The early absolute order is scale dependent. Relative normalization is performed for each direction and each pair, not by dividing an aggregate RMS by maximum local deflection. 

Moderate dye mismatch (log SD .5, additive intensity noise .02, exposure xi) yields matched-2 mV signed score .03895/.05045 mV at one step; at noise .05 mV, four pairs, ideal local controls, conditional single-age association counts 16/15, two-sided age contrast 450 clusters per age. At two steps .00573/.01561 mV and 188/41 single-age counts; three-step association is much weaker. At half variation, one-step single-age counts 166/99. No observed biological effect or validated animal count is claimed. Common-noise planning includes the variance of the sampled weights under sampling without replacement. 

## Verification
Max linear residual 9.999e-11, max reciprocity discrepancy 2.47e-11. Independent dense projected solves on 7^3 sites agree within 1.50e-11 relative, tighter 31^3 solves within 6.45e-12; tracer uniformization agrees with direct exponentiation within 4.08e-14 absolute. All five designs have complete age vectors for the declared ages. The large-scale assay is an electrical projection, not a 29,791-site full multi-ionic EDNM simulation. The native age trajectories, ablation, exact proofs and adverse structural/rival results of v0.17 are unchanged.
