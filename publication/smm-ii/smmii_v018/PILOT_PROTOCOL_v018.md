# CA1 paired-recording pilot — SMM-II v0.18

## Distinct estimands
The full-operator P9–P12 window is a conditional prediction of the two-conductor and native reference models. It is not reproduced by the relative focal transfer in the new 3D weighted-graph assay. The first pilot tests geometry-associated transfer **within** age; it does not use a P11>P21 pair-transfer criterion as a proxy for the theorem. A separate, independently shunt-calibrated or spatially spanning design would be required to test the full normalized operator or the native neuronal-correlation contrast. No validated matched-budget biological manipulation is assumed.

## Calibration and design
- P11 and P21 CA1 passive astrocytes, independently sampled animals and litters; block by litter and preparation. Characterize P9–P12 secondarily.
- Record resting voltage, cell phenotype, both endpoint input resistances, pair positions and spacing, access resistance, depth, reference/bath configuration. A 9.7 MOhm passive-astrocyte group resistance is the Zhong P10–12 anchor, not a measured resistance at each day. Test target deflections and actual measured age differences in the pilot.
- Test ±100, ±200, ±400 pA current steps for linearity and stable response, with predeclared exclusion of non-linear or unstable responses. 200 pA at 9.7 MOhm gives 1.94 mV; matched 2 mV requires about 206 pA, or 333 pA at 6 MOhm. These are design values, not a validated stimulation protocol.
- Use both directions and report raw transfer impedance, fixed-current mV and remote/local ratios. Axial pairs at one, two or three estimated intercellular spacings are prespecified, not optimized after inspecting outcomes. Record actual Euclidean distance and tissue geometry; lattice steps are model categories.
- Use fixed loading and imaging conditions for tracer spread (e.g. biocytin) and independently calibrate permeability versus electrical transfer, with local resistance and positions as nuisance variables. Dye alone does not identify the conductance graph.

## Quantitative planning targets
Primary 64 synthetic 31^3-site networks, 32 held out, matched local 2 mV: adjacent-pair residual RMS 0.0670 mV (P11) and 0.0888 mV (P21); axial two-step 0.0134/0.0278 mV; three-step 0.00228/0.00815 mV. With the moderate tracer-mismatch scenario (edge log-SD 0.5, fluorescence noise 0.02 donor units), adjacent signed mean score 0.0390/0.0504 mV. At four distinct pairs per graph and independent residual voltage noise SD 0.05 mV, illustrative single-age positive association counts 16/15 independent synthetic graph clusters. Comparing the small age difference needs about 450 clusters per age. Halving weight variation raises single-age adjacent counts to 166/99. These calculations assume the trained predictor generalizes and local controls are exact. They are not power estimates for animals or litters; use pilot animal/litter variance, actual tracer reliability, recording yield and a minimum meaningful score to design confirmation. Do not regard either age ordering as established in tissue.

## Confirmatory gates
Predeclare the prediction and signed held-out score using independent training/pilot animals; test geometry association within each age. Use animal/litter-level intervals and multiplicity control for secondary distances. If independently verified geometry engagement and measurement reliability hold, an upper confidence limit below a prespecified minimum positive score defeats that magnitude; failed engagement or unreliable measurement is inconclusive. A positive association alone neither demonstrates the full-operator window nor establishes developmental architecture. For the latter two, use the separate native correlation/template and causal architecture protocols in the manuscript.
