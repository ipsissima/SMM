The frozen subject-level reference was read from `amended_subject_results.csv`; the original optimizer directional records were read from `amended_optimizer_diagnostics.csv`. The sub-044 four directional optimizer scores and overall scores are bitwise identical. For sub-075 and sub-608, the M2 optimizer explains nearly all numerical score variance; M3 scores match very closely. All per-subject signs remain negative.

Original versus rerun quality decisions are identical for sub-075 and sub-608: good epoch counts, bad channel list, ICA exclusion component set and rejected epoch indices. Changes in ICLabel probabilities are at most 5.96e-8 for sub-075 and 1.55e-6 for sub-608. The actual clean-epoch arrays for sub-608 differ with a relative Frobenius norm of 5.438e-9, a maximum single sample discrepancy of 3.638e-12 V, i.e. preprocessing numerical roundoff rather than a different signal selection.

The optimizer's L-BFGS iteration counts differ on rerun (especially M2), compatible with numerical sensitivity; **the exact cause has not been proved** (requires controlled refit of original FIF and regenerated FIF in the same environment). Do not claim bitwise identical cohort-wide reproducibility on this evidence.

No attempt is made to transform these three audit subjects into a new confirmatory statistical test. The full cohort of 535 has not been rerun.

Local supplementary artifacts include a reproducibility comparison script, CSVs, original and new QC comparisons, and exact new fit JSON for all three subjects. Only diagnostic metadata and aggregate fit values are retained in this repo audit; raw EEG epochs are not committed.
