# SMM — Genealogy from the Original Brain-Mesh Model to the Definitive Reconstruction
## What is preserved, what changes, and why the theory remains the same research programme

**Canonical comparison date:** 2026-10-05  
**Versions compared:** arXiv:2412.12106v1 (29 Nov 2024), arXiv:2412.12106v3 (15 May 2026), published Frontiers control-field paper (2026), definitive SMM reconstruction.

---

# 1. Executive conclusion

The definitive SMM is **not a cosmetic revision of SMM v1**.

Relative to v1, it changes:

- the ontology of the mesh;
- the spatial extent of direct mesh coupling;
- the mathematical dynamics of the mesh;
- the neural population model;
- the status of phase;
- the cross-layer coupling mechanism;
- the interpretation of low-frequency modes;
- the role of plasticity;
- the empirical validation strategy.

However, it preserves the deepest research-programme claim:

> Brain-wide neuronal organization may depend on a spatially distributed, non-synaptic, slower layer of tissue dynamics that is not reducible to the long-range neuronal connectome and that changes the conditions under which neuronal coherence, metastability and plasticity occur.

Thus the **mechanism is reconstructed**, while the **central explanatory hypothesis is preserved**.

# 2. SMM v1: what the original theory actually was

The first public version was titled:

> **The Brain-Mesh Model: A Unified Framework for Neural Synchrony, Plasticity, and Coherence**

Its conceptual order was:

1. identify phenomena supposedly insufficiently captured by standard connectivity models;
2. postulate a new all-brain mesh-like connectivity layer;
3. model this layer by analogy with a metallic elastic mesh;
4. derive waves, interference, resonance and distributed plasticity;
5. only later ask what biological substrate might implement such a mesh;
6. propose astrocytic networks as the strongest candidate biological correlate.

The original mesh therefore had an **analogy-first ontology**.

Its defining equations included:

\[
F_{ij}=-k_{ij}\Delta L_{ij},
\]

and

\[
\partial_t^2u=c^2\nabla^2u+\eta,
\]

with harmonic frequencies

\[
f_n=\frac{nc}{2L}.
\]

The model then coupled this mesh variable directly into both a neural-mass layer and a Kuramoto phase layer:

\[
\dot E_i=\cdots+\gamma u_i,
\]

\[
\dot\theta_i=\omega_i+\sum_jK_{ij}\sin(\theta_j-\theta_i)+\kappa u_i.
\]

Plasticity also received a direct mesh covariance term.

Astrocytes entered later as a proposed statistically/functionally equivalent implementation of the already-defined metallic-mesh dynamics.

# 3. The principal conceptual shift

## v1 direction of explanation

\[
\boxed{
\text{desired global phenomena}
\rightarrow
\text{postulated mesh physics}
\rightarrow
\text{candidate astrocytic realization}
}
\]

## definitive direction of explanation

\[
\boxed{
\text{astrocyte physiology}
\rightarrow
\text{multiscale reduction}
\rightarrow
\text{effective mesh dynamics}
\rightarrow
\text{test against global phenomena}
}
\]

This reversal is the most important methodological change.

The definitive SMM does **not** ask astrocytes to instantiate a pre-selected mathematical analogy.

It asks:

> What effective mesoscale operator is actually produced by known astrocytic syncytial physiology?

The answer is then tested against EEG and strong null models.

# 5. What is genuinely lost from the original SMM

The following are not merely implementation details. They are claims of v1 that the definitive theory abandons.

## 5.1 A new global connectivity network

v1 postulated an all-brain mesh capable of transmitting effects independently of ordinary structural connectivity.

The definitive SMM does not.

Instead:

\[
\text{local astroglial control}
\rightarrow
\text{local neuronal state}
\rightarrow
\text{long-range neuronal connectome}.
\]

Therefore a distant effect does not imply direct centimeter-scale astroglial transmission.

## 5.2 Elasticity as physical principle

Hooke-law elasticity was constitutive in v1.

It has no privileged role in the definitive SMM.

## 5.3 Intrinsic mesh waves as carriers of coherence

v1 treated wave interference as the mechanism that directly generated global coherence.

The definitive SMM treats the glial layer as dissipative slow control.

## 5.4 Fixed harmonic resonance as a signature

The original prediction

\[
f_n=\frac{nc}{2L}
\]

is not part of the definitive core.

## 5.5 Connectivity-independent phase propagation in the strong sense

The definitive SMM does not promise that large-scale phase gradients bypass all neuronal connectivity.

It predicts that local neuronal dynamical parameters are modulated by a non-neuronal spatial field, which can change phase organization produced by the neuronal network.

## 5.6 Direct mesh-induced plasticity

The original covariance rule

\[
\Delta C_{ij}\supset
\alpha_M\,{\rm Cov}(u_i,u_j)
\]

has no protected status.

Plasticity becomes an extension requiring an explicit cellular mechanism.

# 6. What is preserved from the original SMM

Despite the losses above, the central intellectual structure survives.

## 6.1 Two geometries

The original theory insisted that neuronal structural connectivity is not the only relevant spatial organization.

The definitive model preserves this exactly:

\[
W_N
\]

is the long-range neuronal connectivity geometry, while

\[
L_g
\]

is the local astroglial syncytial geometry.

They are physically and mathematically distinct.

## 6.2 A slow non-neuronal degree of freedom

The original mesh supplied a distributed dynamical state not reducible to synaptic weights.

The definitive astroglial state does the same.

## 6.3 Modulation rather than replacement of neuronal dynamics

This became explicit in v3 and is canonical in the published Frontiers paper.

The electrical EEG generator remains neuronal.

The glial state modifies the dynamical landscape in which neuronal modes evolve.

## 6.4 State-dependent global organization

The original SMM aimed to explain why the same structural brain could express different patterns of coherence.

The definitive model provides a more physical mechanism:

\[
A_0
\rightarrow
A(g),
\]

where the slow astroglial state \(g\) changes the effective neuronal dynamical operator without requiring structural rewiring.

## 6.5 Non-synaptic contribution to coordination

The definitive theory still asserts that extracellular/astroglial physiology can alter network organization without itself being a synaptic or axonal pathway.

## 6.6 Task/rest and perturbational predictions

v1 already proposed that the mesh contribution should vary with brain state and stimulation.

The definitive theory retains this family of predictions, but translates them into homeostatic-state and recovery dynamics rather than interference physics.

# 7. Why v3 and the published Frontiers paper matter

SMM v3 had already moved far away from v1.

It explicitly reframed the astrocytic layer as a **phenomenological mesoscale control field** that shapes rather than directly generates electrophysiological activity.

The published Frontiers paper completed the conceptual transition:

- EEG/MEG currents are primarily neuronal;
- astrocytes need not oscillate at the frequency they regulate;
- glial control fields modulate gain, damping, coupling, excitability and stability;
- a later technical paper is supposed to provide the full derivation, simulations, inference, null models and empirical validation.

Therefore the definitive reconstruction is **much more different from v1 than from the published theory**.

A useful genealogy is:

# 8. The strongest continuity with v1

The deepest original move was **not the wave equation**.

It was the claim that:

\[
\boxed{
\text{structural connectivity}
\neq
\text{complete dynamical geometry of the brain}.
}
\]

The definitive SMM retains this, but replaces the speculative missing geometry with a measurable candidate:

\[
\boxed{
\text{neuronal connectivity geometry}
+
\text{astroglial homeostatic control geometry}.
}
\]

This is a stronger and more falsifiable form of the original intuition.

---

# 9. The biggest cost of the reconstruction

The definitive model is less able to promise spectacular effects a priori.

It no longer guarantees:

- brain-wide non-synaptic waves;
- fixed cross-subject harmonics;
- large mesh effects during ordinary resting activity;
- direct distributed plasticity.

Those are now empirical questions.

In particular, current physiological evidence suggests that gap-junction-dependent spatial K buffering may become especially important during sustained or strong activity, while local uptake mechanisms can dominate under milder conditions.

Therefore a genuinely possible outcome is:

\[
\text{strong post-task / high-load SMM effect}
\]

but

\[
\text{small resting-state SMM effect}.
\]

That would be scientifically informative rather than a failure of analysis.

---

# 10. The biggest gain

The definitive theory can fail quantitatively.

This is a gain.

It will have hard bounds on:

- physiological \(K_e\);
- glial uptake kinetics;
- gap-junction transport;
- neuronal K sensitivity;
- timescale separation;
- parameter identifiability.

If fitting EEG requires values outside those bounds, the theory fails.

The original SMM had too much freedom to reinterpret its field.

The definitive SMM will have much less freedom and therefore much greater evidential value if it survives.

The relationship to the original theory should be described as:
